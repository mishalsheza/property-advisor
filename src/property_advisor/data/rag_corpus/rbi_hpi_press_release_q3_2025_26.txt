All-India House Price Index for Q3:2025-26

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) compiles the House Price Index (HPI) on a quarterly
basis using transaction-level data received from the registration authorities. Today, the
RBI released 1 the HPI for Q3:2025-26 with base year as 2022-23, covering eighteen
major cities 2.
Highlights:
- The House Price Index rose to 115.6 in Q3:2025-26, from 114.2 in the previous
quarter, driven by rise in housing prices across major cities – Jaipur, Kanpur and
Chennai – reflecting a quarter-on-quarter growth of 1.2 per cent (Chart 1)
- All India HPI grew by 3.6 per cent year-on-year in Q3:2025-26, compared with a
growth of 6.9 per cent in the same quarter last year. The increase was primarily driven
by cities such as Nagpur, Chandigarh, and Jaipur (Chart 1).
