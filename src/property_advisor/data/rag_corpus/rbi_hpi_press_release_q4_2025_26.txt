May 29, 2026
All-India House Price Index for Q4:2025-26

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) compiles the House Price Index (HPI) on a quarterly
basis using transaction-level data received from the registration authorities. Today, the
RBI released 1 the HPI for Q4:2025-26 with base year as 2022-23, covering eighteen
major cities 2.

Highlights:

- The House Price Index rose to 115.9 in Q4:2025-26, from 115.6 in the previous
quarter, driven by rise in housing prices across cities viz – Jaipur, Lucknow and Pune,
reflecting a quarter-on-quarter growth of 0.2 per cent (Chart 1).
- All India HPI Year-on-Year growth registered as 4.2 per cent in Q4:2025-26,
compared with a growth of 3.8 per cent in the same quarter last year. The increase
was primarily driven by cities such as Nagpur, Jaipur, Chandigarh and Kanpur (Chart
1).
