August 24, 2026
All-India House Price Index for Q1:2026-27

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) compiles the House Price Index (HPI) on a quarterly
basis using transaction-level data received from the registration authorities. Today, the
RBI released 1 the HPI for Q1:2026-27 with base year as 2022-23, covering eighteen
major cities 2.

Highlights:

- The All India HPI increased by 1.1 per cent and stood at 117.5 in Q1:2026-27
compared to previous quarter, primarily driven by an increase in the index for cities
such as Chandigarh, Lucknow, and Thiruvananthapuram (Chart 1).
- The HPI registered an annual growth of 3.6 per cent in Q1:2026-27, same as in
Q1 of previous year, mainly contributed by the cities such as Chandigarh, Jaipur,
Kanpur, Lucknow and Thiruvananthapuram (Chart 1).
